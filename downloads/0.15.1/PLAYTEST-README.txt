COGNITIVE TERRAIN · PLAYTEST BUILD 0.15.1

Thanks for playtesting. Pick ONE of the options below.

WINDOWS 10/11 (CognitiveTerrain-0.15.1-Setup.exe)
  1. Double-click the file. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the
     build is not code-signed yet: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  2. The game installs itself (no admin rights needed), adds "Cognitive Terrain" to the
     Start Menu and Desktop, and opens in its own window.
  3. To remove it: Start Menu > "Uninstall Cognitive Terrain".

MAC (CognitiveTerrain-0.15.1-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip for M1/M2/M3/M4, -Intel.zip for older Macs)
  1. Unzip and drag "Cognitive Terrain" into Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click the app > Open > Open. If macOS says it is damaged or
     cannot be verified, open Terminal and run:
        xattr -cr "/Applications/Cognitive Terrain.app"
     then open it again.
  3. Opens in Chrome or Edge as an app window if you have one, otherwise in Safari.

LINUX (CognitiveTerrain-0.15.1-Linux.tar.gz)
  tar xzf CognitiveTerrain-0.15.1-Linux.tar.gz && ./cognitive-terrain-0.15.1-linux/cognitive-terrain

ANY COMPUTER (cognitive-terrain-playtest-0.15.1.html)
  Double-click the HTML file. It opens in your browser. Nothing to install.

TWO PLAYERS
  No account, server or internet game service is needed. You swap short text codes.
  1. Blue: on the start screen choose TWO PLAYERS > NEW GAME AS BLUE. Play your moves,
     press END TURN, then COPY CODE and send it to the other player (email, chat, Teams).
  2. Olvana: choose TWO PLAYERS, paste Blue's first code, press JOIN AS OLVANA.
     Blue's moves play out on your screen, then you take your turn and send a code back.
  3. Keep swapping codes until week 16. Each player's game saves after every code:
     close it any time and pick RESUME under TWO PLAYERS.
  The two players can use different builds (exe, Mac app, HTML file) as long as both are the same version.

NOTES
  - Works offline. Fonts look nicer when you are online.
  - Your settings and story history are saved in the browser the game opens in.
  - Please note: which scenario and difficulty you played, whether you won,
    anything confusing, and anything that felt repetitive.
