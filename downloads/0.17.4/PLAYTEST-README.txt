COGNITIVE TERRAIN · PLAYTEST BUILD 0.17.4

Thanks for playtesting. Pick ONE of the options below.

WINDOWS 10/11 (CognitiveTerrain-0.17.4-Setup.exe)
  1. Double-click the file. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the
     build is not code-signed yet: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  2. The game installs itself (no admin rights needed), adds "Cognitive Terrain" to the
     Start Menu and Desktop, and opens in its own window.
  3. To remove it: Start Menu > "Uninstall Cognitive Terrain".

MAC (CognitiveTerrain-0.17.4-macOS-AppleSilicon.zip for M1/M2/M3/M4, -Intel.zip for older Macs)
  1. Unzip and drag "Cognitive Terrain" into Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click the app > Open > Open. If macOS says it is damaged or
     cannot be verified, open Terminal and run:
        xattr -cr "/Applications/Cognitive Terrain.app"
     then open it again.
  3. Opens as an app window in Chrome, Edge, Chromium or Brave if you have one,
     otherwise in your default browser.

LINUX (CognitiveTerrain-0.17.4-Linux.tar.gz)
  tar xzf CognitiveTerrain-0.17.4-Linux.tar.gz && ./cognitive-terrain-0.17.4-linux/cognitive-terrain

ANY COMPUTER (cognitive-terrain-0.17.4.html)
  Double-click the HTML file. It opens in your browser. Nothing to install.

TWO PLAYERS
  No account, server or internet game service is needed. You swap short text codes.
  1. Blue: on the start screen choose TWO PLAYERS > NEW GAME AS BLUE. Play your moves,
     press END TURN · GET CODE, then COPY CODE and send it to the other player
     (email, chat, Teams).
  2. Red (Olvana or Donovia, set by the campaign): choose TWO PLAYERS, paste Blue's
     first code, press JOIN AS RED.
     Blue's moves play out on your screen, then you take your turn and send a code back.
  3. Keep swapping codes until week 16. Each player's game saves after every code:
     close it any time and pick RESUME under TWO PLAYERS.
  The two players can use different builds (exe, Mac app, HTML file) as long as both
  are on the same minor version (for example 0.17 with 0.17.1). The game cannot
  check this for you.

STRUCTURED SESSIONS (WITH A WHITE CELL)
  A facilitator can join as a third person, the White Cell, who sees both sides and
  can steer the game once a week, after Red and before Blue.
  1. Blue chooses TWO PLAYERS > NEW STRUCTURED SESSION · WITH WHITE CELL.
  2. Red joins with Blue's first code as usual, but sends its code to the White Cell.
  3. The White Cell chooses TWO PLAYERS, pastes Red's first code, presses JOIN AS WHITE CELL.
  Codes then go Blue > Red > White Cell > Blue. The Practitioner's Manual explains more.

IMMERSIVE BRIEFINGS (OPTIONAL)
  A free voice pack (CognitiveTerrain-Voice-Emma.zip, on the playtest Releases page)
  reads each week in review aloud, offline. Load it in Settings > Immersive Briefings,
  or put the zip beside an installed game.

NOTES
  - Works offline. Fonts look nicer when you are online.
  - Your settings and story history are saved in the browser the game opens in.
  - Please note: which campaign and difficulty you played, whether you won,
    anything confusing, and anything that felt repetitive.
